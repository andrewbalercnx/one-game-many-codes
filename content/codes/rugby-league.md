Rugby league split from union in 1895 over broken-time payments, then kept diverging on
the field. Its defining move came in 1966: a limit on tackles. Once possession was finite,
every other habit of the modern game — the play-the-ball, the kick on the last, the
kick-chase — followed from the scarcity.
